# TikTok Video Script: "My Portfolio Talks to Recruiters"

## 60-Second Script (Conversational & Engaging)

---

## 🎬 HOOK OPTIONS (Choose One - First 3 seconds)

### Option 1 (Curiosity):
**Visual:** Screen recording of the AI agent orb, voice speaking
**What to say:**
> "What if your portfolio could literally talk to recruiters? In your own voice?"

### Option 2 (Shock):
**Visual:** Screen recording of conversation happening
**What to say:**
> "I built an AI that interviews for me. And it's actually working."

### Option 3 (Problem-Solution):
**Visual:** Quick cut of resume, then portfolio
**What to say:**
> "Recruiters get hundreds of resumes. So I built something that talks to them instead."

### Option 4 (Personal):
**Visual:** Your face or screen recording
**What to say:**
> "My portfolio can now have conversations with recruiters. Here's what happened."

---

## 📝 MAIN SCRIPT (3-60 seconds)

### PART 1: The Problem (3-10 seconds)
**What to show:**
- Quick screen recording of your portfolio
- Or a quick shot of a resume document

**What to say:**
> "I'm an AI and Data Engineer. I was tired of sending out resumes and hoping recruiters would read them. So I thought - what if my portfolio could actually talk to them?"

---

### PART 2: The Solution (10-25 seconds)
**What to show:**
- Screen recording of clicking "Talk to John" button
- The 3D orb animating
- Voice conversation starting

**What to say:**
> "I built a voice AI agent that lives on my portfolio. When someone visits, they can literally have a conversation with it. It answers questions about my experience, my projects, everything. And it sounds like me."

---

### PART 3: The Demo (25-50 seconds)
**What to show:**
- Screen recording of you asking a question
- The agent responding with voice
- Maybe show it searching for information
- Show the conversation flowing naturally

**What to say:**
> "Watch this - I ask it about my finance experience. [Pause for response] See? It found my projects, explained what I did, and it sounds natural. The best part? It automatically saves who visited and what they asked about, so I can follow up."

---

### PART 4: The Value (50-55 seconds)
**What to show:**
- Quick cut showing visitor information being saved
- Or show multiple conversations

**What to say:**
> "So now, instead of just hoping someone reads my resume, I know exactly who's interested and what they want to know. It's like having a 24/7 networking assistant."

---

### PART 5: Call to Action (55-60 seconds)
**What to show:**
- Your portfolio website URL
- Text overlay with "Try it yourself" and "Comment TUTORIAL"

**What to say:**
> "Want to try it? Link in my bio. And if you want me to show you how I built it, just comment 'TUTORIAL' and I'll make a full breakdown video."

---

## 🎥 WHAT TO DO (Step-by-Step)

### Setup:
1. **Record screen** - Use OBS, QuickTime, or built-in screen recorder
2. **Prepare demo** - Have your portfolio open and ready
3. **Test audio** - Make sure your voice is clear
4. **Choose hook** - Pick one hook option that feels natural to you

### While Recording:
1. **Start with hook** (3 seconds)
   - Show the orb or conversation
   - Say your chosen hook
   
2. **Show the problem** (7 seconds)
   - Quick cut of resume or portfolio
   - Explain the problem naturally
   
3. **Introduce solution** (15 seconds)
   - Click the "Talk to John" button
   - Show the orb animating
   - Explain what it does in simple terms
   
4. **Live demo** (25 seconds)
   - Ask a real question
   - Let the agent respond
   - Show it working naturally
   - Mention the visitor tracking feature
   
5. **Wrap up** (10 seconds)
   - Show the website URL
   - Give clear call to action
   - Invite them to comment for tutorial

### Editing:
1. **Add text overlays** - Key points (not too technical)
2. **Add background music** - Trending TikTok sound
3. **Add subtitles** - For accessibility
4. **Quick cuts** - Keep it fast-paced
5. **Zoom effects** - Zoom in on important parts

---

## 💬 CONVERSATIONAL TIPS

### Do's:
✅ Speak naturally, like you're telling a friend
✅ Use simple words (avoid jargon)
✅ Show excitement about your project
✅ Keep it personal ("I built this because...")
✅ Make it relatable ("We all send resumes...")

### Don'ts:
❌ Don't list technical specs
❌ Don't use complex terminology
❌ Don't sound like you're reading a script
❌ Don't go into too much detail
❌ Don't make it sound like an ad
